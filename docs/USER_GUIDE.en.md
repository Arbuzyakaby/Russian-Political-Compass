## Getting started

On your first visit you choose a language, then the site offers a **quick start**: five steps over the live page, from the field to the book. The **full tour** (twelve steps) adds convocations, analytics, the face-off, Cast your own votes, the command palette, export and settings. Both are in **Settings → General → Guided tour**, in the command palette (Ctrl+K) and in the update window. Arrow keys move between steps, Esc leaves; if you reload the page mid-tour, it carries on from the same step.

The English version lives at `/en/`; `?lang=en` on any address works too.

## The four sections

- **Compass.** Two axes from −10 to +10: economics left to right, state power bottom to top. A solid dot is a party's actual position, a hollow one its stated position. Tap a dot for the party card. Below the field: the make-up of the chamber, seats over time, the spectrum, the sub-axis radar and a coalition sandbox.
- **Quiz.** 20, 40 or 90 statements on one shared scale. The result is a point on the same field and a list of parties by distance. Answers stay in your browser.
- **Votes.** 44 bills and each faction's position at the final reading. Every card links to the Duma's voting record and the bill page. *Cast your own votes* asks you the same questions; you can mark a bill as important (it counts double), see how each faction voted right after you answer, undo the last answer and copy a link to your answers. Opening someone's link never overwrites your answers without asking.
- **About.** A short book. One chapter per screen, *Previous/Next* at the bottom, contents in the sidebar on a wide screen or in a bottom sheet on a phone, search across all chapters, a bookmark of the last chapter you read and printing of a chapter or the whole book. Old links such as `#ab-axes` open the matching chapter.

## Face-off

Pick two parties and, if you like, one convocation. You get the distance on the field, the axes and sub-axes side by side, agreement overall, by topic (economy, state and rights, foreign policy) and by convocation, a one-line summary and the bills on which the factions disagreed. The address `#/duel/er-kprf?c=8` opens the same view.

## Your data

Settings, quiz answers, Cast-your-own-votes answers, the book bookmark and tour progress are kept in your browser's localStorage under keys starting with `pc-`. **Settings → Privacy** lists them and has a button that erases everything; **Settings → General** downloads or restores a backup file. Nothing is sent to a server.
